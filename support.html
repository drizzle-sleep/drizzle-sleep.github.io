FAQ

Does Drizzle play with the screen locked or alongside other apps?
Yes. Drizzle keeps playing with the screen locked and when you switch apps. With Snore detection on, it can play alongside music or videos from other apps and keep listening. With Snore detection off, another app's audio pauses Drizzle; press play again to bring it back.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings → Drizzle Lifetime → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

What happens after the 30-day trial?
Gentle Rain, Heavy Rain and Rain on a Tent stay free. Drizzle Lifetime, a one-time purchase, unlocks everything. Your saved mixes are never deleted.

Snore detection doesn't record.
Allow microphone access in iOS Settings → Drizzle. Turn on Snore detection in Drizzle's Settings, open the Snores tab, and tap Start when you go to bed. Nights are recorded first; sounds are classified in the morning. Clips stay on your iPhone.

What do the Snores clips show?
Each night starts collapsed and shows its three loudest clips. Tap the night to expand and hear every clip. Tossing and turning is labelled Tossing.

Does Drizzle tap my Apple Watch when I snore?
No. Apple Watch snore alerts have been removed. Snore detection still records on your iPhone overnight; clips appear in Snores in the morning.

Play together can't find the other phone.
Open Play together on the Routine tab on both iPhones, keep them close and unlocked, and allow Local Network access.

Can I cast sounds to a TV or speaker?
Yes. AirPlay and Chromecast buttons sit on the playback bar next to the sleep timer.

Suggest a sound or function
Use “Suggest a sound or function” in Drizzle's Settings, or open the feedback form in your browser.
