FAQ

Does Drizzle play with the screen locked or alongside other apps?
Yes. Drizzle keeps playing with the screen locked and when you switch apps. With Snore detection on, it can play alongside music or videos from other apps and keep listening. With Snore detection off, another app's audio pauses Drizzle; press play again to bring it back.

How does the free launch offer work?
Right now, every scene and sound in Drizzle is free for everyone who downloads the app.

Everyone who joins now becomes an early supporter and keeps full access for good.

There are no subscriptions or ads, and your saved mixes are kept.

Snore detection doesn't record.
First, make sure Drizzle has microphone permission in your iPhone's Settings.

In Drizzle, the Snore detection switch is in Settings. You can turn it on there, or go to the Routine tab and tap Start under “Ready to sleep?”; tapping Start turns on Snore detection automatically.

The separate Snore alerts switch is also under “Ready to sleep?”. Alerts are optional and are not required to record clips.

Listening begins after your sleep sounds stop, such as when the sleep timer ends. Sound classification happens on your iPhone, and short clips are saved there for review in the morning. Nothing is uploaded.

If no clips appear, check microphone permission and make sure you tapped Start when you went to bed.

What do the Snores clips show?
Each night starts collapsed and shows its three loudest clips. Tap the night to expand and hear every clip. Tossing and turning is labelled Tossing.

Does Drizzle tap my Apple Watch when I snore?
Yes, if you turn on Snore alerts under “Ready to sleep?” on the Routine tab.

When Drizzle hears snoring for about 4 seconds, it can send a notification to your iPhone, at most once every 2 minutes. If your iPhone is locked and you're wearing your Apple Watch, iOS may pass the notification to the Watch, making it buzz. When your iPhone is unlocked, the alert appears on your iPhone instead.

This is an iPhone notification mirrored by iOS, not a separate snore detector or custom vibration running on the Watch. Other loud noises can occasionally trigger an alert. Short night-sound clips stay on your iPhone for the morning.

Play together can't find another device.
On the playback bar, tap the Play together icon below AirPlay. Turn on Play together on each device, then tap a nearby device to request a connection. The other device must accept.

Keep Drizzle open on each iPhone or iPad, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled and Local Network access allowed. Devices should be nearby, but they do not need to be connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Up to 8 devices can play together. Each keeps its own volume, and nothing is sent to the internet. Playback starts are closely aligned but not sample-accurate, and timing can drift over a long night.

Can I play sounds on a TV or speaker?
Yes. Tap AirPlay on the playback bar, next to the sleep timer and Play together. AirPlay lets you play your Drizzle mix through a compatible speaker or Apple TV.
