FAQ

Does Drizzle play with the screen locked or alongside other apps?
Yes. Drizzle keeps playing with the screen locked and alongside other apps.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings → Drizzle Lifetime → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

What happens after the 30-day trial?
Gentle Rain, Heavy Rain and Rain on a Tent stay free. Drizzle Lifetime, a one-time purchase, unlocks everything. Your saved mixes are never deleted.

Snore detection doesn't record.
Allow microphone access in iOS Settings → Drizzle, turn on Snore detection in Drizzle's Settings, and start a mix.

The Apple Watch doesn't tap me.
Allow notifications for Drizzle, wear your Watch, and lock the iPhone. Alerts go to the Watch when the iPhone is locked.

Two-phone mode can't find the other phone.
Keep both iPhones close and unlocked, with Drizzle open on both, and allow Local Network access.

Suggest a sound
Use "Suggest a sound" in Drizzle's Settings.
