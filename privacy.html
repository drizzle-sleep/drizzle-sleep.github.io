Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Drizzle is made by Kata Lara Molnár. Drizzle does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The developer receives nothing from the app.

Accounts, ads and data

Drizzle does not require an account and does not show ads.

Drizzle's current app privacy declaration reports no collected data and no tracking. Night-sound clips and sound classification stay on your iPhone. Your recordings are not uploaded.

Microphone

Drizzle uses the microphone only when Snore detection is on. Listening begins after your sleep sounds stop. Sounds are classified on your iPhone using Apple's built-in on-device sound classifier, which also powers optional Snore alerts. Short clips are saved only on your iPhone for you to review in the morning, and you can delete them at any time. Audio is never uploaded.

Location

Drizzle requests When In Use location permission with approximate (reduced) accuracy only if you choose the Sunset to sunrise schedule for Red night. Your approximate location stays on your iPhone and is used only to recalculate sunset and sunrise times; it is never uploaded or shared, and the developer receives no location data. Custom hours do not use location, and you can turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time.

Night-sound labels

Drizzle does not use your iPhone's motion sensors to detect tossing or turning. When Snore detection is enabled, labels such as “Tossing” are inferred from sounds, such as rustling.

Sound classification happens on your iPhone. Short clips are stored locally and are not uploaded.

Play together

Play together allows up to 8 nearby iPhones and iPads to play the same mix over local connections. Each device keeps its own volume.

The devices need Drizzle open, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled, and Local Network permission. They do not need to share the same Wi-Fi network.

Connected devices can change the mix, timer, or evening Routine, and the others follow the latest change. Playback starts are closely aligned but not sample-accurate, and timing may drift over a long night.

Play together communication remains local. Nothing is sent to the internet or to the developer.

Notifications and alarms

Bedtime reminders and the system wake alarm are scheduled on your device. Snore alerts are optional and use sound detection on your iPhone.

If your iPhone is locked and you're wearing an Apple Watch, iOS may mirror a snore notification to the Watch, which can vibrate. The Watch does not independently listen for or classify snoring.

Suggest a sound or function

This option opens a Google Form in your browser. Anything you enter there is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Backups

Your Drizzle data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's terms cover.

Children

Drizzle does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
