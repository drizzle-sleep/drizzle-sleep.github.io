Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Drizzle is made by Kata Lara Molnár. Drizzle does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The developer receives nothing from the app.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Drizzle has no sign-in and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Microphone

Drizzle uses the microphone only if you turn on Snore detection. Apple's built-in sound classifier recognises snoring and other sounds on your device during the night. Short clips are saved only on your iPhone, and you can delete them at any time. Audio is never uploaded.

Motion

While Snore detection is on, the iPhone's motion sensor is used on the device to notice tossing and turning. This information is not sent anywhere.

Two-phone mode and Play together

Two nearby iPhones exchange data directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth: the mix to play, or loudness information used to work out who snored. None of it goes over the internet or to the developer.

Apple Watch

Your iPhone and Apple Watch send playback and alert messages directly to each other.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders, the wake alarm and snore alerts are scheduled on your device.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Suggest a sound

This option opens a Google Form in your browser. Anything you enter there is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Backups

Your Drizzle data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's terms cover.

Children

Drizzle does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
