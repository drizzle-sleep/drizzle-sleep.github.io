Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Drizzle is made by Kata Lara Molnár. Drizzle does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The developer receives nothing from the app.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

Drizzle has no sign-in and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Microphone

Drizzle uses the microphone only if you turn on Snore detection. Nights are recorded on your device first; sounds are classified in the morning with Apple's built-in on-device sound classifier. Short clips are saved only on your iPhone, and you can delete them at any time. Audio is never uploaded.

Motion

While Snore detection is on, the iPhone's motion sensor is used on the device to notice tossing and turning (labelled Tossing). This information is not sent anywhere.

Play together

Two nearby iPhones can exchange the mix to play directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. None of it goes over the internet or to the developer.

Notifications and alarms

Bedtime reminders, the wake alarm and similar notifications are scheduled on your device. Drizzle does not send Apple Watch snore-alert taps.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Suggest a sound or function

This option opens a Google Form in your browser. Anything you enter there is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Backups

Your Drizzle data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's terms cover.

Children

Drizzle does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
